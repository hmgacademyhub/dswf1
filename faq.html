❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about DataFlow Studio v14.0 — HMG Academy Edition.

Is DataFlow really free?

Yes. The 1-day trial is completely free with all 70 modules unlocked. No payment, no credit card, no AI API costs. The platform runs entirely in your browser.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation required. Just open the URL in any modern browser. You can optionally install as a PWA for offline access.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data never leaves your browser. All processing happens client-side using JavaScript.

Why "No AI API"?

For data analysis, rule-based algorithms are deterministic, free, private, fast, and reproducible — better than AI APIs. We use them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The PWA service worker caches everything. Data persists in IndexedDB.

What files can I download?

You can generate and download: .ipynb (Jupyter Notebook), .py (Python script), .csv, .json, .xlsx, .tsv, .html, .md, .pdf, Dockerfile, requirements.txt, environment.yml, and more. See the Report Builder module.
